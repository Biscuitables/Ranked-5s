## Ranked 5s Ban Advisor v1.6.2

Free champ select scouting for League of Legends (Windows 10 / 11).

**Download:** Ranked5sBanAdvisor.zip below → right-click → Extract All → open Ranked5sBanAdvisor.exe.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't code-signed. Click **More info** → **Run anyway**.

- Suggested bans, enemy strengths and weaknesses, premade detection
- Lock-in study and enemy jungle pathing
- Porofessor tags at the loading screen, win tracking
- Optional Riot API key support
- Chinese, Japanese and Korean name support
